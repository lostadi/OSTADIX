# Development credit and provenance

Lee Daghlar Ostadi is the project maintainer. Record contributor credit
accurately and preserve existing attribution, upstream license notices and
historical authorship.

CI and the tracked pre-push hook run the
[metadata checker](../scripts/check_commit_authors.py). It inspects Git,
Cargo and citation fields; it does not verify identity ownership, provenance
truth or test execution.

OSTADIX uses AI coding agents for implementation, investigation and review.
[The development disclosure](AI_ASSISTED_DEVELOPMENT.md) distinguishes that
assistance from design responsibility and observed validation. Factual
`Generated-By`, `Assisted-By`, `Reviewed-By`, `Tested-By`, and session records
can document how a change was produced. Preserve actual tool assistance and
review records.

A tool review is not a human review, an authored test is not evidence of
execution, and a byline is not proof of correctness. Keep review and execution
evidence specific to the artifact and environment tested. Do not invent
contributor credit, sign-offs, test results or human review. Keep credentials
and private transcripts out of public records.
