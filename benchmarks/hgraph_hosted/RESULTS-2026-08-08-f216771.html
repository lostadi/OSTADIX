Interpretation and non-claims

This record demonstrates wait concurrency on a multicore machine. It neither recreates nor claims a one-logical-CPU result, and it is not evidence of CPU parallel computation. Worker capacity is not CPU affinity or a processor reservation.

The analyzer predicts static hosted-task layers. It does not predict durations, reserve CPU or memory, prove runtime readiness, choose observed placement, or prove that a legal layer overlapped physically. The measurement validates the prediction against this bounded fixture/runtime/machine combination.

Semantic equivalence covers returned values only. It excludes filesystem and network effects, scope snapshots, traces, timing, and external-effect ordering. The record does not widen explicit-autonomous-unordered into strict serial effect equivalence and does not claim general application throughput or scheduler optimality.
